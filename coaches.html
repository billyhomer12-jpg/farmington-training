Coaches

Local. Hands-on. Every rep.

Billy Homer, 26

Born: Littleton, Colorado

Billy has had a basketball in his hands since before first grade and played all the way through his junior year of high school, plus years of AAU ball that took him on the road to compete. Getting cut his senior year didn’t end his love for the game. It fueled it, and he kept playing intramurals in college. He also grew up playing football, baseball, and soccer, so he knows what it takes to build a complete athlete. Growing up coached by his dad is a big part of where his passion comes from, and two years into coaching, he’s paying it forward with Desert Storm, a Farmington AAU travel team, helping every kid he trains get better one rep at a time.

D

Devin Collins, 26

Born: Aurora, Colorado

Devin Collins played college basketball for multiple seasons, competing at a level that taught him what real preparation, discipline, and work ethic look like. Born in Aurora, Colorado, he brings that college-level experience straight to the gym in Farmington. With three years of coaching under his belt, he also leads Desert Storm, a local AAU travel team, alongside Billy, and loves helping young players build the skills and confidence to reach the next level.

Where we train

Every package is four 2-hour sessions (8 hours total). Prices are for the whole package; small-group prices are for the whole group. Gym sessions follow San Juan College Health & Human Performance Center hours and closures.
