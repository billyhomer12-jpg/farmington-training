Coaches

Local. Hands-on. Every rep.

Billy Homer, 26

Born: Littleton, Colorado

Billy has had a basketball in his hands since before first grade and played all the way through his junior year of high school, plus years of AAU ball that took him on the road to compete. Getting cut his senior year didn’t end his love for the game. It fueled it, and he kept playing intramurals in college. He also grew up playing football, baseball, and soccer, so he knows what it takes to build a complete athlete. Growing up coached by his dad is a big part of where his passion comes from, and two years into coaching, he’s paying it forward with Desert Storm, a Farmington AAU travel team, helping every kid he trains get better one rep at a time.

Devin Collins, 26

Born: Aurora, Colorado

Devin Collins is a former collegiate forward and high school coach who brings discipline, structure, and a player-first mindset to training. A 6'5" forward from Aurora, Colorado and Rangeview High School, Devin played at Dawson Community College, where he was a Co-Captain, All-Conference 2nd Team selection, and Mon-Dak Player of the Week. After Dawson, he signed to play at Simon Fraser University (NCAA Division II). He finished his college career at Ottawa University Arizona, earning his B.A. in Psychology in 2023, before transitioning to coaching at Piedra Vista High School, where he has coached since 2024. He also coaches Desert Storm, a Farmington AAU travel team, alongside Billy.

Where we train

Sessions are 2 hours. Packages are four 2-hour sessions (8 hours total); a single 1-on-1 session is one 2-hour session. Prices are for the whole package; small-group prices are for the whole group. Gym sessions follow San Juan College Health & Human Performance Center hours and closures.
