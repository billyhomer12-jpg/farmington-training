Privacy

Privacy Policy

Plain language, no fine print. Effective October 15, 2026.

What we collect

When you sign up or book, we collect only what we need to run training:

  • Parent and athlete names and the athlete's age.
  • Contact info: parent email and phone number.
  • Booking details: package, programs, workout type, session times, and any message you send us.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. You enter your card on Stripe's secure checkout, and we never see or store your card number. Stripe tells us only whether a payment went through.

Email

Booking confirmations, reminders and schedule changes are sent through Resend, our email delivery service. It receives your email address and the message content only to deliver our emails.

How we use it

To schedule and confirm sessions, contact you about your athlete's training, handle gym closures and rescheduling, and keep basic business records.

We never sell your information

We don't sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't use it for outside advertising. We share it only with the services above, and only so they can do their job for us.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Contact

Questions? Email fourcornershoopsandperformance@gmail.com.

Effective date: October 15, 2026. If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date.
