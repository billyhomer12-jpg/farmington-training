Membership terms

Monthly memberships are month to month, with no lock-in. They renew automatically each month until you cancel.

  • Cancel anytime in the billing portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid month: you keep access through that date and you are not billed again.
  • No partial refunds for the month in progress.
  • No pausing. Memberships can be canceled but not paused.
  • Up to 2 unused sessions roll into the next month only, then expire. Sessions blocked by gym closures roll over outside that cap. Unused sessions expire when a canceled membership ends.
  • Picking dates: you pick your session dates when you sign up. 4-session plans pick all 4; Monthly Plus picks 4 at sign-up and the other 4 before the start of week 3 of each billing month.
  • Rescheduling: 1 reschedule per billing month, at least 24 hours before the session, using your reschedule link. It resets when your membership renews. A second reschedule in the same month isn't available.
  • Members can book up to 4 weeks ahead. If a payment fails, new bookings are paused until the payment goes through.

Sessions are 2 hours. Packages are four 2-hour sessions (8 hours total); a single 1-on-1 session is one 2-hour session. Prices are for the whole package; small-group prices are for the whole group. Gym sessions follow San Juan College Health & Human Performance Center hours and closures.
