Open groups

Solo parents pay $125 per athlete and pick one of these groups. Mon/Wed/Fri groups hold 2–3 players, Tue/Thu groups hold 4. A group needs 2 paid players; 48 hours before the first session, under-filled groups email their parents (move or refund) and show Refund/move needed here. Refunds are done by hand in Stripe. A group's times block 1-on-1 and own-group bookings only once it has a paid player; empty groups leave the slot open, and if someone books it the group is hidden from parents. Unpaid spots are held for 1 hour.
