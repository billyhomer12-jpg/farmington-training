FAQ

Is strength training safe for kids?

Yes, when it's supervised and technique-first. We start with bodyweight and light loads and progress only when form is solid.

What should my athlete bring?

Court shoes, athletic clothes, a water bottle and a basketball if they have one.

Can parents watch?

Yes, parents are welcome to watch sessions.
