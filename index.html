Is $250 for one session?

No. $250 is for a package of four 2-hour sessions (1-on-1). Small group prices are also for four 2-hour sessions, total for the group.

How do I pick my 4 sessions?

In booking, choose your workout type, then tap 4 start times on any days you like. Gym sessions must end before the HHPC closes.

What if a time doesn't work for the coach?

We'll confirm each session or suggest another time before you pay.
