Frequently Asked Questions

Is the 1-on-1 package price for one session?

No. $250 $200Was $250, now $200 is the 1-on-1 package of 4 sessions, 2 hours each (8 hours total). If you want just one session, the Single 1-on-1 Session is $75 $60Was $75, now $60 for one 2-hour session. Small group packages are a total for the whole group: $300 $240Was $300, now $240 for 2 players, $350 $280Was $350, now $280 for 3, $375 $300Was $375, now $300 for 4, also 4 two-hour sessions. Joining a group solo is $125 $100Was $125, now $100 for one player.

Can my kid join a group if we don't know other families?

Yes. Choose Join a group solo when you book. You pay $125 $100Was $125, now $100 for just your athlete (4 two-hour sessions) and pick an open group, and each group shows how many spots are left. Monday, Wednesday and Friday groups have 2–3 players; Tuesday and Thursday groups have up to 4. A group runs once it has 2 players. If it doesn't have 2 players two days before the first session, we'll email you to move to another group or get a full refund.

Can I book just one session?

Yes. The Single 1-on-1 Session is one 2-hour private session for $75 $60Was $75, now $60. Choose it on the booking page, pick one time, and pay. It's a great way to try us out before buying a 4-session package.

How do I pick my sessions?

After you choose a package and enter your info on the booking page, pick your two-hour times (1 for a single session, 4 for a package) from the open slots on the calendar, review them, then pay securely by card or Apple Pay. You're booked once payment goes through.

What if a time doesn't work?

You choose and pay for the sessions you want. If something changes later, use the reschedule link in your confirmation email to move a session yourself, up to 24 hours before it starts. Can't find the email? Check your spam or junk folder.

How do I reach you?

Call or text (505) 469-3851, or email fourcornershoopsandperformance@gmail.com. We usually reply within a day.

Can we use the HHPC weight room?

No. The San Juan College HHPC weight room is for ages 13+ and the gym’s own trainers only, so Four Corners sessions don’t use it.

Do I need to sign a waiver?

Yes, and it's all online. When you book, the parent or guardian signs the waiver on the booking page before paying. Every player needs their own parent's signature: for a group, you sign for your athlete and we give you a link to share so each other family signs for theirs. A signed copy is emailed to you. You can read the waiver here first.

How do I cancel a monthly membership?

Memberships are month to month with no lock-in. Cancel anytime in the billing portal (Manage/Cancel on your dashboard). You keep access and can use your sessions through the end of your paid month, and you won't be billed again. There are no partial refunds, and memberships can't be paused.

Can I reschedule a membership session?

Yes, once per billing month. Use your reschedule link at least 24 hours before the session. Your one reschedule resets when your membership renews.
